How it works

  1. Discover — finds named businesses (cafés, restaurants, salons, shops…) via OpenStreetMap around your chosen area.
  2. Enrich — visits each business website to find a contact email automatically.
  3. Quote — estimates card turnover from the business type, local area, competition density and opening pattern, then prices it on the tier ladder. Floors: 0.4% rate and £15/month rental for the highest earners.
  4. Route — each lead lands in the right queue: Email (has an address — approve or auto-send), Call (phone only — your team calls and logs the outcome), or Letter (no contact details — print & post via Stannp or manually).
